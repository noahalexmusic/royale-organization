Organization leadership

Meet our executive team.

Founded and led together by husband and wife Noah and Scarlett, The Royale Organization is built around shared vision, teamwork, and a passion for the Clash Royale community.

NA
Founder & CEO • Crownbound Leader

Noah Alex CR

As Founder & CEO, Noah established The Royale Organization to bring Clash Royale players, creators, and independent clans together under one supportive community. He leads its overall vision, competitive plans, partnerships, and long-term growth. Alongside his wife and co-owner Scarlett, he helps turn new ideas into real opportunities for members while keeping teamwork, fairness, and loyalty to the game at the center of TRO.

Crownbound Creator Profile TikTok Twitch YouTube
S
Co-Owner • Executive Leadership

Scarlett

As Co-Owner and Noah's wife, Scarlett leads alongside him in shaping the future of The Royale Organization. She contributes to major decisions, long-term planning, partnerships, and the experience members have within the community. Together, Noah and Scarlett guide the organization's direction, support its leadership team, and work to create a welcoming place where clans, players, and creators can grow.

RG
Organization Manager

RG

As Organization Manager, RG helps coordinate daily operations, clan recruitment, communication, and community development. He works closely with Noah and Scarlett to support partnered clans, keep projects organized, and help turn leadership plans into action.

L
Advertiser

Levi

As Advertiser, Levi helps more people discover The Royale Organization through outreach and promotion. His role focuses on raising awareness, showcasing the community, and connecting interested players, content creators, and clan leaders with TRO.
