Community Standards

Code of Conduct

Respect, fair play, and accountability across The Royale Organization.

The Royale Organization (TRO) aims to provide a welcoming, competitive Clash Royale community. These standards describe the behavior expected of players, clan leaders, creators, coaches, staff, and anyone representing TRO.

1. Respect Everyone

  • Treat members, opponents, volunteers, and community partners with respect.
  • Do not harass, threaten, bully, discriminate against, or deliberately humiliate others.
  • Disagreements are allowed; personal attacks and targeted abuse are not.

2. Play Fair

  • Follow Clash Royale rules and the published rules for each TRO event.
  • Do not cheat, exploit matches, impersonate others, or manipulate tournament outcomes.
  • Respect referees and event decisions; raise disputes through appropriate channels.

3. Represent TRO Professionally

  • Communicate responsibly when acting on behalf of TRO.
  • Do not make unauthorized promises about sponsorships, payments, prizes, or partnerships.
  • Respect the privacy of members and avoid sharing private messages or personal information without permission.

4. Clan Partnerships and Independence

Partnered clans retain their own identities and leadership. Clan representatives should communicate honestly, disclose relevant external partnerships, and follow any separately agreed partnership terms. These standards do not replace an individual clan's rules.

5. Creators, Coaches, and Staff

  • Present qualifications, affiliations, and services accurately.
  • Honor commitments and communicate promptly when plans change.
  • Do not pressure members for money, personal information, or special treatment.
  • Staff and coaches must maintain appropriate professional boundaries with members, especially minors.

6. Reporting Concerns

Members can report concerns to TRO leadership or email contact@theroyaleorganization.com. Include a description of what happened and relevant evidence when safe to share. TRO aims to review reports fairly, respect privacy, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.

7. Responding to Violations

Depending on severity and context, TRO may provide guidance, issue a warning, restrict participation, remove a role, suspend access, or end a membership or partnership. Serious safety concerns may require immediate protective action. People affected should have an opportunity to explain their perspective when appropriate.

8. Keeping Standards Current

TRO may update these standards as the organization grows. Specific tournament rules, creator agreements, and partnership terms may contain additional requirements. Material policy changes should be communicated to affected members.

Need help or want to report an issue?

Contact TRO leadership at contact@theroyaleorganization.com. For general questions, visit our FAQ & Help Center.
