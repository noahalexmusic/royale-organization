Introductory sponsorship options for businesses and brands interested in supporting our growing esports community.

Community supporter

Bronze

$25 / month

Best for: Small businesses, local brands, and supporters who want to help a growing Clash Royale community while receiving public recognition.

Bronze is our entry-level partnership. Your contribution helps TRO work toward community events, player development, and tournament operations. In return, visitors can discover your brand through our sponsor showcase.

  • Sponsor listing: Your approved business name and logo appear in a dedicated sponsor area on the TRO website during your active sponsorship.
  • Clickable business link: Visitors can follow a link from your listing to your approved website or landing page.
  • Bronze recognition: Your listing identifies your business as a Bronze community supporter.

Example: A small gaming accessories shop could use Bronze to introduce its brand to visitors interested in Clash Royale and esports.

Ask About Bronze
Community visibility

Silver

$75 / month

Best for: Businesses that want stronger recognition and a coordinated promotional opportunity beyond a standard website listing.

Silver includes all Bronze benefits, with more prominent website positioning and one approved community announcement per month. This gives your business an additional opportunity to explain what it offers to the TRO community.

  • Everything in Bronze: Logo, business link, and sponsor recognition remain included.
  • Priority showcase position: Your listing appears ahead of standard Bronze listings in the sponsor area.
  • Monthly community announcement: We coordinate one approved sponsor announcement per month through an agreed TRO community channel, subject to channel availability and rules.

Example: A gaming service or equipment brand could introduce a new product through a clearly labeled sponsor announcement.

Ask About Silver
Featured partnership

Gold

$150 / month

Best for: Brands seeking a more prominent relationship with TRO and opportunities to connect their name with our esports activities.

Gold is our highest introductory tier. Alongside Silver benefits, your business receives featured website recognition and a regular opportunity to discuss upcoming events, campaign ideas, and sponsorship performance with leadership.

  • Everything in Silver: Includes the website listing, priority position, and one approved monthly community announcement.
  • Featured website placement: A more prominent, clearly labeled sponsor feature on an agreed TRO website section.
  • Event sponsorship discussion: Priority opportunity to discuss appropriate recognition around future tournaments or events; event branding is not included unless separately agreed.
  • Monthly check-in: A conversation with TRO leadership to review delivered promotions and plan the next month.

Example: A larger gaming brand could become a featured website sponsor and explore supporting a future TRO championship.

Ask About Gold

These are introductory monthly sponsorship offers, not automatic purchases. Sponsor listings and featured placements will be activated after approval and agreement. We confirm the exact pages, channels, creative materials, dates, renewal terms, and deliverables in writing before payment. Sponsorship helps support TRO activities, but funds are allocated according to organizational needs and approved budgets. We do not guarantee audience size, impressions, clicks, customers, or sales.
