November 2026 • King vs. King season

New Evolutions: Giant & Mirror

Two new card Evolutions arrive in November. These are new abilities, not balance buffs or nerfs.

👊
1 cycle • 5 Elixir base card

Giant Evolution — Rage Trail

Once the evolved Giant reaches 50% health, he becomes enraged: his movement and attack speed increase, and he leaves Rage areas behind him to boost supporting troops. The Rage trail expands as he lands more attacks, making him a powerful front-line tank for beatdown pushes.

Unlock: Evolution Shards or a premium Pass Royale choice reward.

🪞
2 cycles • Mirrored card costs 1 extra Elixir

Mirror Evolution — Evolved Double

When evolved, Mirror copies the last card you played as its Evolution, one level higher, provided that card has an Evolution. The original card does not need to occupy an Evolution slot or have its Evolution unlocked. Cards without an Evolution are copied normally.

Unlock: Evolution Shards or the Royal Market from November 9.

Source: Supercell's official November season announcement. Availability and gameplay details may change with future updates.
