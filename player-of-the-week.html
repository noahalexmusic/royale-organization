Who Can Win?

All active TRO members are eligible, including players, creators, coaches, and partnered clan members. Awards recognize contributions as well as competitive achievements.

Clan leaders can nominate deserving members. TRO leadership reviews nominations and the Founder/CEO and Manager RG make the final decision together.

How Winners Are Chosen

40% Performance & Improvement

Ranked progress, Trophy Road improvement, tournament results, and growth relative to personal goals.

25% Activity & Dedication

Consistent participation in TRO, events, and team activities.

20% Sportsmanship

Respect for opponents and teammates, helpfulness, and responsible conduct.

15% Community Contribution

Coaching, creating content, welcoming members, and strengthening the organization.

The percentages are evaluation guidelines, not an automated ranking. High trophy counts alone do not guarantee a win.
