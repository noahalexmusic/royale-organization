Creator Program

Build your audience, collaborate with other creators, and contribute to the TRO community.

TRO welcomes Clash Royale streamers and content creators at different stages of their journey. The program is designed to connect creators with the community while recognizing consistent, positive contributions.

Community Creators

A place for emerging creators who want to connect, collaborate, and grow.

  • Community networking
  • Opportunities for collaborations
  • Potential promotion through TRO channels

Established & Active Creators

Creators with a consistent track record may be considered for additional responsibilities and support.

  • More prominent feature opportunities
  • Potential event and content collaborations
  • Additional perks considered individually

What We Look For

  • Clash Royale content or streams that contribute to the community
  • Professional, respectful behavior toward viewers and other members
  • Honest communication about activity and availability
  • Willingness to collaborate and represent TRO positively

Applications are reviewed individually. A particular follower count or perk is not guaranteed by this page.

Perks & Support

Depending on activity, contribution, available funding, and individual agreements, TRO may offer promotion, collaboration opportunities, and creator benefits. Any paid perks or Pass Royale support must be explicitly confirmed by leadership; they are not automatic entitlements.

Expectations

Creators should follow TRO's Code of Conduct, disclose relevant external affiliations, and honor any individually agreed exclusivity or promotion requirements. Specific obligations will be discussed before acceptance.

How to Join

Visit the Join TRO page to explore current application options. Include your platforms, content focus, and what you hope to contribute. Leadership will review applications and follow up where appropriate.

Important: This page describes the program, not a promise of sponsorship, compensation, or guaranteed promotion. Confirmed benefits and responsibilities are agreed individually.
