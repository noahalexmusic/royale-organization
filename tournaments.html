How to enter

Registration flow

1

Watch for the announcement

Confirmed tournament details will appear here and in the Royale Organization Discord.

2

Register before the deadline

Follow the official signup instructions published for that event. Registration is not open until the event is confirmed.

3

Check in and compete

Registered players follow the event schedule, match instructions, and administrator directions.

4

Results are verified

Compete with honor

Core competition rules

Event-specific settings may change, but every Royale Organization tournament is built around fair play, clear administration, and respect.

⚔

Match format

Format, scoring, deck restrictions, match settings, and advancement rules are announced before each event.

📋

Eligibility

Players must complete the official registration process and meet any event-specific eligibility requirements before the deadline.

🤝

Fair play

Cheating, exploiting, harassment, intentional disruption, and unsportsmanlike conduct are prohibited.

🛡

Admin decisions

Tournament staff review disputes and verify results. Event-specific procedures will be posted with the official rules.

⏱

Check-in

Players are responsible for following announced check-in times and being ready when their match is called.

🏆

Prizes

Prize amounts, eligibility, and distribution details are announced for each tournament before registration closes.
