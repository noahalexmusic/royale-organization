Tournament Rules & Guidelines

Know what to expect before competing in a TRO Clash Royale event.

Important: Event rules are not yet finalized.

This page explains TRO's intended approach to tournaments. Registration requirements, schedules, prizes, match settings, and binding event rules will be confirmed in each tournament's official announcement. Until then, this page is informational.

TRO aims to host fair, organized, welcoming Clash Royale competitions. Players should review the specific event announcement before entering because formats and eligibility can vary.

1. Registration & Eligibility

  • Players must follow the eligibility requirements listed for their specific event.
  • Use accurate registration information and a valid Clash Royale player identity.
  • Any check-in deadlines, roster restrictions, or regional limitations will be stated in the event announcement.

2. Expected Player Conduct

  • Play fairly and follow Supercell's applicable rules.
  • No cheating, intentional match manipulation, account impersonation, harassment, or abuse toward opponents or officials.
  • All participants are expected to follow TRO's Code of Conduct.

3. Match Formats

TRO is planning events that may use best-of-three (Bo3) or best-of-five (Bo5) series. For the proposed seasonal championship structure, the round of 16 and quarterfinals would use Bo3, while semifinals and the grand final would use Bo5. These are plans, not finalized rules for any particular event.

Deck restrictions, bans, game modes, tiebreakers, and bracket procedures will be specified separately for each tournament.

4. Scheduling & Check-In

Event announcements will provide the official start time, check-in instructions, match reporting process, and any lateness or no-show procedures. Participants should monitor the designated event communication channel for updates.

5. Reporting Results & Disputes

Players should follow the event's result-reporting instructions and preserve relevant screenshots when possible. If a match result or ruling is disputed, contact the designated tournament official promptly. The event-specific rules will explain deadlines and how decisions or appeals are handled.

6. Disconnects & Technical Problems

Connection failures and technical issues should be reported to the tournament team as soon as possible. Remakes, forfeits, or other remedies are not automatic and will depend on the published event rules and available evidence.

7. Prizes & Event Changes

Prize amounts, eligibility, payment arrangements, and any sponsor conditions will be published only when confirmed. TRO may need to change or cancel an event; participants will be notified through official channels where practicable.

Preparing for future events

TRO is working toward its first official tournament in December 2026. No specific date, registration window, or prize is confirmed on this page.
