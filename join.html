Clan partnership process

From application to official partner.

Clan applications are reviewed before any clan is represented as an official member of The Royale Organization.

01

Submit the application

Tell us about your clan, leadership, members, activity, goals, and why you want to join the organization.

02

Leadership review

Organization leadership reviews the application and decides whether the clan is a good fit for the community and its standards.

03

30-day probation

Accepted clans enter a 30-day trial period. The clan remains responsible for showing activity, teamwork, communication, and commitment.

04

Final review

At the end of probation, leadership reviews the clan's progress and decides whether to approve the partnership.

05

Official integration

Approved clans can be fully integrated into the organization, including their official website clan page and community structure.

What we look for

A good partnership goes both ways.

🤝

Teamwork

Leaders and members who communicate, support each other, and work with the wider organization.

📈

Activity & Growth

A clan that wants to stay active, improve, recruit responsibly, and build for the long term.

⚖

Respect

Fair leadership, competitive integrity, and respect for players, staff, opponents, and the community.
