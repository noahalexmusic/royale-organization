Building our organization

How clan applications work

Every clan joining The Royale Organization goes through a review process designed to protect our community and support long-term growth.

1

Submit your application

Clan leaders provide information about their clan, leadership, members, and goals.

2

Leadership review

Our leadership team reviews the application and determines whether the clan is a good fit.

3

30-day probation

Approved applicants enter a 30-day probation period to demonstrate teamwork, activity, and commitment to our values.

4

Official partnership

After successful completion and final leadership approval, the clan becomes a partner of The Royale Organization.
