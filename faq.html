Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about joining, competing, creating, and partnering with The Royale Organization.

Joining TRO

Is it free to join The Royale Organization?

Yes. There is no fee to apply or join TRO as a member or partnered clan.

How do I apply?

Visit our Join TRO page and choose the application path that fits you.

Do applications get approved automatically?

No. Applications are reviewed by TRO leadership before acceptance.

Partnered Clans

Does my clan need a minimum number of members?

No. TRO does not require a minimum clan size to apply.

Will I lose control of my clan?

No. Partnered clans keep their own identity and leadership while participating in the broader TRO community.

Is there a trial period?

Yes. New clan partnerships include a 14-day trial period and leadership review.

Can my clan have other partnerships?

External partnerships must be disclosed during the application and partnership process. TRO leadership will review compatibility with existing commitments.

Coaching & Creators

Can I apply to coach Clash Royale players?

Yes. Visit the Coaching page to learn about coaching and submit an application.

How does paid coaching revenue work?

Approved coaches set their proposed session prices, subject to review. For completed paid sessions, coaches receive 80% and TRO receives 20%.

Can streamers and creators join TRO?

Yes. TRO welcomes creators interested in contributing to the Clash Royale community. Visit Streams & Creators and Join TRO for available opportunities.

Tournaments & Sponsorships

Where can I find tournament announcements?

Official event details will be published on the Tournament Hub and News & Announcements page once confirmed.

How can a business sponsor TRO?

See our Sponsors & Advertising page for packages and the sponsor inquiry form.

Still have questions?

Connect with the TRO community on Discord or email contact@theroyaleorganization.com.
