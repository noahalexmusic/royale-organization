Streamer Development & Growth Team

Help build a supportive future for Clash Royale creators.

We are planning a team to support creators through personalized 90-day growth plans, content strategy, collaboration opportunities, and progress reviews. You do not need to be a competitive Clash Royale player to express interest.

Current status: expressions of interest only. These roles are not yet confirmed openings. TRO is reviewing whether and how unpaid participation can legally be offered. No employment, compensation, or appointment is promised. We will explain the terms of any opportunity before inviting anyone to participate.

INITIAL PRIORITY

Director of Creator Development

Lead creator onboarding, individualized 90-day plans, staff coordination, and monthly reports to TRO leadership.

  • Organization and communication skills
  • Experience supporting creators or teams preferred
  • Estimated availability: 5–7 hours/week
INITIAL PRIORITY

Social Media & Growth Manager

Advise on TikTok, YouTube, and Twitch strategies, content schedules, engagement, and creator analytics.

  • Content strategy and platform knowledge
  • Ability to give constructive feedback
  • Estimated availability: 4–6 hours/week
FUTURE OPPORTUNITY

Video Editor & Graphic Designer

Create stream highlights, short-form clips, thumbnails, and TRO promotional materials.

  • Portfolio or sample edits helpful
  • Comfortable with editing or design software
  • Estimated availability: 3–5 hours/week
FUTURE OPPORTUNITY

Creator Events Coordinator

Plan creator collaborations, joint livestreams, community events, and cross-promotion with TRO tournament staff.

  • Scheduling and communication skills
  • Interest in esports communities
  • Estimated availability: 3–5 hours/week
