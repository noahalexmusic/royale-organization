Coach-set pricing

Coaches choose their own rates.

Each approved coach sets their session price and duration. For every completed paid session, the coach earns 80% and The Royale Organization receives 20%. Rates will be clearly shown before booking, and TRO reviews rates before publishing them.

Coach sets the price

Each coach proposes their own price and session length.

Coach earns 80%

For a $25 session, the coach receives $20.

TRO earns 20%

For that same $25 session, TRO receives $5 for supporting the coaching program.
